## Adopted Opaque Math Transport
For the bounded [runtime](./math.md#adopted-opaque-client-runtime), `render::opaque_math_fragments` borrows ParsedComments and emits one fragment per real Doc8 occurrence in block/occurrence source order. It joins only MathRange interior segments without rewriting payload; original attachments, target/publication, outer range and discontiguous maps remain with the caller under those same ordinals. Unavailable input or malformed segment bounds refuse; available-empty remains empty. This is neither a page renderer nor a serialized provenance schema.
Each inline `span` or display `div` carries attributes in fixed order: class, data-mizar-block, data-mizar-occurrence, data-mizar-display, data-mizar-tex. Unpadded decimal ordinals and lowercase hexadecimal UTF-8 payload bytes avoid HTML parsing/entity/newline transformations, including NUL. A role=status child and code child provide a visible pending fallback. Escape HTML metacharacters and spell non-HTML-preservable controls as visible `\u{hex}` there; the hex attribute remains exact. Client fallback uses textContent with the decoded original payload. No payload enters executable source, innerHTML, URLs or attributes other than hex.
The local asset handoff exposes fixed relative `assets/katex/` paths and immutable bytes, validates the complete exact manifest/configuration set before integration, and refuses missing/extra/changed entries. The standalone client derives its local base from its own script URL, including nested pages; it verifies runtime/CSS/fonts/configuration against pinned digests before loading them, rejects redirects/foreign origins and retains safe fallback on failure. Verified asset bytes alone supply executable/CSS/font blob URLs. Site staging/publication and complete HTML templates remain gated.

## Joint Q4/MT Summary Definition
This bounded consumer definition resolves Q4's degraded module/search text projection under adopted [Doc8 recovery](./commonmark.md#degradation-and-adoption-boundary) and [MT precedence](../../../spec/en/22.error_handling_and_diagnostics.md#2269-documentation-markdown-and-tag-warnings). ### Selection And Recovery
Bind input to the same full-provenance target/publication and its complete source-ordered attachment list. In each block whose Doc8 view and maps completed, traverse ordinary CommonMark paragraphs in source order, including structural paragraphs in containers. Exclude recognized tag paragraphs using Doc8 recognition, including known tags with missing fields or duplicate occurrences; unknown/lookalike tags remain ordinary text. Do not reinterpret headings, code/HTML blocks, reference definitions or display math as paragraphs. Select the first eligible paragraph across these blocks before flattening, even if its flattened text is empty; never seek a later nonempty result.
An actual conversion/map failure excludes that whole block's failed view from selection. Do not reparse, split, flatten or treat preserved raw fallback as an ordinary paragraph. Continue scanning successful neighbors in their original order. The degraded summary is therefore the first eligible *successful-view* paragraph, without claiming to recover a failed block's first paragraph. Retain the complete raw failed block, original block identity/maps/provenance and existing W0411 behavior with the documentation input/full-comment presentation; an empty or later summary does not erase that failure. MT owns warning occurrences, retained condition details, locations, ordering and promotion; no summary-specific warning or status prefix is added. A failed block after the selected paragraph cannot change its text; failures before it cannot hide a successful neighbor.

### Exact Text And States
Flatten the selected paragraph by Q4's existing rules: text/code to visible text, link labels and image alternative text to their text, math to its unchanged supplied payload, soft/hard breaks to spaces and raw HTML tokens to their literal spelling. Collapse ASCII whitespace to one space and trim it, without Unicode normalization or truncation. This is plain text: context escaping occurs only at the final HTML/JSON sink, never by extracting text from rendered HTML. Math runtime success, link resolution or highlighting cannot change summary text.
Known absence of a comment yields null for search and empty visible module-summary text. Available comments with no eligible successful paragraph yield `""`, including tag-only, non-paragraph-only and all-failed inputs; all-failed remains raw recovery, never known absence. Available empty text, or a selected paragraph flattening to empty, also yields `""`. Unavailable required attachment/order/target/view-state inputs are neither absence nor available-empty: preserve the existing readiness boundary. An owner-established failed block with retained raw fallback is available recovered input, not such missing metadata. Lower-reader integrity/schema/currentness refusals remain errors.

## Adopted HTML And URL Output Policy
This bounded Q5 adoption governs output only; CommonMark recognition, raw/interpreted destination storage and Doc10 classification/resolution remain unchanged. Raw HTML tokens, including inline tags, render as visible context-escaped literal spelling without a warning. No remote asset/image fetch occurs during generation.
For an ordinary URL-class link or image, apply this predicate to the exact CommonMark-interpreted destination, never its raw spelling: reject any ASCII C0 control (U+0000–001F), DEL or backslash anywhere; ignore leading/trailing U+0020 only for checking. In the remaining string, if a colon precedes every slash, question mark and hash (absent delimiters count as later), require an ASCII scheme matching `[A-Za-z][A-Za-z0-9+.-]*` and ASCII-case-insensitively equal to `http`, `https` or `mailto`. Otherwise accept it as relative/fragment, including empty and network-path destinations. Do not entity-decode or percent-decode again, normalize, repair or rewrite the stored destination. Accepted href/src values preserve the interpreted spelling with attribute-context escaping.
Thus `HTTPS://host`, `mailto:a@b`, `../a`, `#x`, `//host/a`, empty and `javascript%3Aalert(1)` pass; `javascript:alert(1)`, `data:x`, `1http:x`, `:x`, ` javaScript:x `, embedded controls and backslashes fail. Entity decoding belongs to CommonMark: `jav&#x61;script:x` is rejected when its interpreted value is `javascript:x`.
Rejected links retain all visible label content without an active href; rejected images retain their visible alternative text without src or an image load. Autolinks retain their visible label. This does not replace a semantic Doc10 reference with a URL or change its lookup. The [URL diagnostic owner](../../mizar-diagnostics/en/documentation_urls.md) defines the single warning; bounded detection/emission is adopted below; full rendering remains gated.

## Q3 Source-View And Line Routing Definition
This bounded Render prerequisite adopts local source presentation/routing only, without complete Doc13 or Q12 diagnostics. It consumes [G3 original-coordinate authentication](./render_prerequisites.md#adopted-g3-source-coordinates), never a normalized hash or independently reopened source file. `AuthenticatedDocSources::original_text` exposes that same retained original text with exact binding checks. `render::DocSourceViews::allocate` joins source views to the complete G2 namespace and retains the immutable sources/inputs; `view` requires the owner-supplied package display name and renders the fixed shell with the authenticated version, and `item_source_slot` composes the authenticated export range through G3. `loaded_source_slot` accepts ordered loaded-frame ranges through the same G3 composition; original segments and mapping coverage remain with that handoff. These operations emit in-memory HTML only.

### Binding And Destinations
Within each selected package/version output root, allocate a view at `sources/` followed by the authenticated packaged relative source path components, appending `.html` to the final component (for example `src/a.miz` becomes `sources/src/a.miz.html`). Bind the allocation to full module identity, publication hash, exact packaged path and G3 original digest. No FQN splitting, host path, source fetch or alternate/native text supplies that binding. Reuse one view only for the identical binding; distinct bindings never merge because their text or path matches.
Declare every source view in G2's complete auxiliary namespace before allocation, checking generated pages, files/directories, reserved assets and ASCII-case conflicts together. Collisions refuse the affected allocation, with no suffix or identity winner. A source view is not a new export/semantic destination. Relative links from any containing page use adopted Q3 per-component percent-UTF8 and full-fragment encoding, followed once by attribute escaping. Never encode the route twice or interpret the packaged path as a URL.

### Original Lines And Segment Links
Compute line starts from the authenticated original UTF-8 text itself: start with zero, then add the offset immediately after each LF. Line numbers are one-based, unpadded decimal; anchors are `L1`, `L2`, etc. This follows Session's LF convention without applying its loaded-text LineMap to original offsets. CRLF advances after LF, lone CR does not advance, empty text has line 1 and trailing LF creates an empty final line. An offset at text length is valid; any offset outside the original text or inside a UTF-8 code point refuses the binding.
An item's Source slot derives only from its artifact `source_range`, through G3 `loaded_ranges` in the authenticated loaded frame. Comment/citation links, if requested by later consumers, use their existing G3 frame-specific composition, never reinterpret lexical/loaded offsets as original. For every returned original half-open segment, link to the line containing its start (the greatest line start no larger than that offset). Preserve segment order, repeats, exact endpoints, source binding and mapping state outside this display projection; do not coalesce segments or create a covering range/highlight. A nonempty segment's exclusive end does not create another link; an authenticated empty segment is a point, including at EOF. No segment means no line link, even when source text is available. Partial mapping retains its known segment links and its incomplete state; missing maps never synthesize offset zero or line 1.

### Source View And Source Slot
Use the existing page shell, with the exact packaged path as `title`, and the bound package/version header. Its BODY is the following literal, using the template expansion/escaping rules below; source views add no search or math hook beyond that shell:
```html
<ol class="mizar-source-lines">{SOURCE-LINES}</ol>
```
SOURCE-LINES concatenates one row per original line: `<li id="L{A:line}"><pre><code>{T:source-line}</code></pre></li>` followed by LF. The line slice excludes its terminal LF only. For visible source text, spell C0 controls other than tab, and DEL, as `\u{hex}` with lowercase unpadded hexadecimal, then apply T escaping once. Thus CR remains visible within its original LF-defined line; source characters never enter HTML, script or highlighting markup. This is a display projection, not rewritten source or a recoverable source-byte serialization; keep the authenticated original bytes/maps unchanged. Empty lines still have anchors. Source rows and their order are independent of exported item or comment enumeration.
The item `source` flow slot is `<p class="mizar-source">Source: {SOURCE-LINKS}{MAPPING}</p>` followed by LF. Each SOURCE-LINKS entry is `<a href="{A:source-href}">line {T:line}</a>`, in segment order, separated by `; `. MAPPING is empty for complete mapping, otherwise exactly ` <span class="mizar-source-mapping">incomplete mapping</span>` when links survive an incomplete map. This does not imply an exhaustive source location or complete reference/citation coverage.
If original source is unavailable, the whole slot is `<p class="mizar-source-unavailable">Source unavailable</p>` followed by LF; allocate no view and preserve the existing warning when source is not packaged, without adding a warning merely for unavailable legacy projection/map metadata. If authenticated text exists but no mapped segment exists, emit `<p class="mizar-source-unavailable">Source location unavailable</p>` followed by LF; a view may exist, but do not invent a line href or treat a map gap as missing source. No new warning, code, location, deduplication, precedence or profile is allocated here; remaining Q12 definitions stay with Diagnostics. Contradictory/stale bindings and lower-reader failures remain refusals, never these unavailable presentations. This prerequisite supplies no Q4/MT, G4/G5, schema/publication or later-gate completion.

## Complete-Page Template Definition
This is a bounded Render-owned G4 definition, not adoption or executable completion. The literals below define package/module/item pages once their owner-supplied inputs are ready. They supply no style/search bytes or immutable asset manifest. [G4 readiness](./render_prerequisites.md#ordered-readiness-gates), coherent Doc13 and all later gates remain separate.

### Expansion And Canonical Bytes
`{T:name}` inserts text; `{A:name}` inserts a double-quoted attribute value. Both replace original `&`, `<`, `>`, `"`, `'` with `&amp;`, `&lt;`, `&gt;`, `&quot;`, `&#39;` respectively, once, without rescanning replacements. Other UTF-8 bytes, including retained payload line endings, are unchanged. This defines generated bytes, not browser DOM round-trip equivalence. `{F:name}` inserts an owner-rendered safe HTML fragment verbatim, never arbitrary authored HTML. `{X}` inserts another expansion defined here. Slot notation is not emitted; slot contents are not template source and undergo no recursive substitution.
All literal lines inside the following fences end in LF, including each row and the closing `</html>`. Expand rows by concatenation with no extra separators; an available-empty row collection expands to zero bytes. Preserve fragment bytes, including their final LF; do not indent, trim, normalize, escape again or serialize through a DOM. Emit UTF-8 without BOM, exactly the shown literal whitespace/attribute order and one final page LF. No timestamps, random ids, absolute paths, locale-dependent strings or browser-generated output enter these bytes.
`T:title` is the authoritative package display name, module display name or exact item FQN for the respective page, and the exact packaged path for a source view; `T:package` is the stored package display name. `VERSION` is empty only for authoritatively absent version, otherwise exactly ` <span class="mizar-version">{T:version}</span>` (including present-empty). This does not add an unversioned Doc4 planner: its selected route still requires a stored version. All href/src slots use allocated destinations relative to the actual containing page with adopted Q3 encoding, then A escaping; no FQN splitting, filesystem guessing, double percent encoding or URL rewriting.

## Package Overview Input Definition
This bounded definition supplies the package template's `{F:package-overview}` input contract only. It adopts no producer, API, serialized field, source syntax or complete-page implementation. ### Selection And Authentication
The owner explicitly selects one complete package overview content product, or explicitly declares that the complete selection contains no overview, for the selected root documentation request. Retain that immutable selection, its owner provenance and the complete content in the owner's representation (including original order and available source/map provenance). The absence declaration covers this explicit selection, not every possible document on disk. No filename/discovery convention is selected here: README, manifest descriptions, source headers, module comments and dependency documents do not implicitly supply or negate package content. A file-leading Doc8 comment remains module-only.
Bind the owner product to the [current selected root plan](./doc_build.md#package-planning): exact package id, stored required version, optional lock identity and publication hash, plus the selected snapshot/request and documentation options. Retain the owner's exact content identity and selection evidence with the payload or absence declaration; a self-reported hash/package tuple authenticates neither. One product must match that selection exactly; duplicate/conflicting, foreign, dependency-only, stale or modified products cannot satisfy the root slot. Do not pick a winner or reuse a same-name/version product across publications or requests.
Only the owner producer may establish a ready handoff by validating the retained selection/content and deriving its complete safe fragment through the owned rendering rules below. Consumers revalidate currentness and exact bindings against retained owner evidence; they cannot manufacture readiness from a caller-provided HTML string, a claimed safe flag, a matching digest or a package plan alone. Changing content, selection, rendering inputs/options or containing-page destinations requires fresh owner derivation/validation. This is an authentication obligation for later implementation, not a new signature/hash domain or an assertion that an existing producer already meets it.

### Safe Flow And Slot States
Available content supplies one complete safe flow fragment derived from all selected content in owner order. Reuse adopted [HTML/URL policy](#adopted-html-and-url-output-policy), [comment safe-flow rules](#adopted-comment-and-tag-safe-flow-handoff) and opaque math transport where applicable: authored HTML is escaped, URL classifications and Doc9 resolution retain their meanings, and adopted raw-block recovery/warnings survive.
