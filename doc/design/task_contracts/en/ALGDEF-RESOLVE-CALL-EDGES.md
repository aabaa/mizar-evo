Prefix functors use their declaration-pattern head; unsupported patterns remain unresolved. Never infer applicability from kind, arity or types.

Exclude actual parameters, block-wide definition binders and source-wide reservations, not type/condition uses. Locals activate at VariableBinding end and stop at their statement-list boundary, flowing only into descendants: initializer i sees completed j<i; own/later initializers retain incoming lookup; shared annotations see completed bindings ([timing](../../mizar-resolve/en/names.md#algorithm-ordinary-variable-occurrences)).
Additional binders: range bounds/step use the incoming environment; the variable binds only the loop annotations/body. Collections are evaluated before introducing element/optional set-only processed names, confined to the loop ([§20.2](../../../spec/en/20.algorithm_and_verification.md#202-control-flow)). Match scrutinees use outer scope; immutable captures bind only their branch; resolved functor/predicate heads remain symbols. Quantifiers bind only their formula ([§4.6](../../../spec/en/04.variables_and_constants.md)). Comprehension generators bind mapper and condition, including a preceding mapper ([owner](../../mizar-resolve/en/names.md#resolver-task-277r2-fraenkel-generator-variable-identity)). Function Binder heads are simultaneous in the body, with head types outside; capture/reconstruction keep their separate modes ([§13.10](../../../spec/en/13.term_expression.md#1310-function-binder--)). Snapshot/Pick add no ordinary exclusions.
Wider admission: assignment RHS and justified return/assert/proof calls retain lexical scope; proof binders are confined to their blocks. Headers identify candidates without authorizing recursive contract use. Qualified heads require the exact current-module namespace, unshadowed at the use; never fall back to the final spelling. Bracket/template heads use their actual declaration pattern; template arguments add no scope. Imports, builtins and unknown names without authenticated borrowed entries remain empty, never fabricated from spelling ([lookup](../../mizar-resolve/en/names.md#scope-model)). Empty means unresolved/unsupported; singleton means only a scope candidate.
