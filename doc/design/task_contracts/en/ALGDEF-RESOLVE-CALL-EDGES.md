Named prefix functors match their actual declaration-pattern head; other entries use their primary spelling. Unsupported functor patterns stay unresolved. Do not infer applicability from symbol kind, arity or types.

Exclude names occurring in the owner's algorithm parameters, its definition-block parameters or source reservations; these syntax exclusions claim no complete local-binding resolution. Headers, qualified/bracket calls, imports, builtins and unknown names retain empty candidate lists. Empty lists mean unresolved or unsupported; a singleton is still only a scope candidate.

Failure: missing/stale/recovered/ambiguous identities remain unresolved and block checked consumers; never resolve from spelling or manufacture a winner. Existing diagnostics/recovery remain owned, with no new numbers.
Forbidden: ordinary forward-reference relaxation, typed-overload decisions in Resolver, termination/proof acceptance, executable loading or guessing call/group provenance.
