## Existing boundary and proposed closure

The [ordinary intermediate consumer](frontend_adapter.md#workspace-intermediate-summaries) validates direct lineage and trusts a current sealed publication for previously consumed ancestors; it does not independently revalidate ancestor payloads. Landed [multiple-direct-leaf caching](multiple_direct_leaf_frontend_caching.md) authenticates every direct leaf/SourceLoad pair: the legacy v1 route in `registry.rs::native_frontend_cache_parents` requires each dependency Frontend to have only root/own SourceLoad graph dependencies, and `frontend_adapter/source.rs::prepare_native_disk_frontend_cache_payload` independently enforces leaf lineage. The intermediate route separately authenticates the complete bounded closure under the implementation contract; the legacy direct-leaf route retains its admission rule.
Under Q1-A, the new closure is exactly importer I → intermediate M → import-free ancestor A, with three distinct modules in one captured workspace package. All three are clean AST-bearing native Disk inputs, with no reexports, recovery or diagnostics; each edge resolves only to the stated target, allowing repeated imports with original ordinal/span/alias/branch provenance. Unrelated workspace modules need not join this closure. Preserve existing zero-/one-/multiple-direct-leaf routes; artifact dependencies/index summaries, cycles/self-imports, extra branches/depth, cross-package intermediates, Open/Generated or unavailable lexical contributions withhold the new optimization. Do not silently trim an inadmissible member.
Keep physical lineage direct: A has its own SourceLoad; M has its own SourceLoad plus A Frontend; I has its own SourceLoad plus M Frontend. PackageResolve is a scheduling prerequisite, not a fabricated payload parent. Ancestors retained/authenticated for caching do not become extra I output parents. At both edges export only complete A13 direct local public contributions, including genuine complete-empty exports; unknown is unavailable. A's symbols never become I-visible implicitly through M. An A edit may change M tokens/keys while M's local exports and I's active lexical environment stay equal; conservative ancestor invalidation below does not change that visibility rule.
Driver owns capture/provider/runtime association and orchestration; Build graph/index/config/lock/readiness; Frontend preprocessing, full keys, parsing/output/storage; Resolver/Artifact resolution/A13 identity; Cache canonical keys/Store; IR physical lineage/reconstruction/retention; Proof acceptance/reuse. Reuse existing private paths and owner representations; no public recursive graph API, summary type or lower-owner seam is approved.

## Currentness, retention and persistence

Validate graph/result/overlay correspondence at both edges; accepted Completed and CacheHit dependencies are equivalent only with real current outputs. Never pre-run a scheduler service or infer readiness from Store presence. A failed/cancelled/missing ancestor cannot prepare a partial hit/write and follows ordinary scheduling.
Acquire scoped IR owners for all three SourceLoads and A/M Frontends before consuming them; acquire the reconstructed I owner on lookup success. Retain the complete consumed closure through expectations, lookup/decode, scheduler acceptance/consumption and final validation. Partial acquisition failure releases already acquired owners; release all on success, miss, refusal, Store/decode/scheduler error, cancellation and supersession. Recheck physical validity, request/lane/snapshot/cancellation/supersession/publisher authority before/after lower work, Store I/O and scheduler handoffs. Retention/read leases grant no current-use/publication right or atomic lifetime across I/O; frozen capture grants no later disk freshness.

Validate complete raw storage before decode, owner semantic/side/named-input expectations, Cache key/produced-by binding, current SourceId rebinding and exact reconstructed direct lineage. Disabled/failing Store, misses or unsupported profiles preserve ordinary results and invalid-input/real-producer error precedence, without new diagnostics or fabricated success. Direct/retained/NoKey routes and later-service/proof/publication/reuse deferrals remain unchanged.

## Owner Q-decisions

**Q1 — profile.** A: exactly the bounded same-package I → M → A closure above, alongside existing routes. B: retain current cache admission and defer intermediate caching. **Recommend A:** it supplies STEP6C's identified prerequisite without authorizing general recursion, branches, artifacts, reexports or cycles.
**Q2 — ancestor evidence.** A: bottom-up independent full-source expectations at every level plus comparison to every accepted dependency payload. B: retain sealed-publication trust and defer intermediate hits until a separately approved owner contract proves a complete independently authenticated ancestor receipt. **Recommend A:** current metadata alone cannot supply independent ancestor payload evidence; B is a deferral, not seal-only admission.
**Q3 — projection.** A: new private intermediate-only v2 closure encoding with existing full owner keys and byte-identical legacy v1 routes. B: defer admission until a separate mapping proves the unchanged v1 direct-leaf encoding commits the entire chain unambiguously. **Recommend A:** explicit relations/ancestor inputs avoid conflating new topology with legacy leaf lists; no public schema change follows.
**Q4 — lifetime.** A: existing Driver readiness callback and scoped IR ownership of the complete bounded closure. B: defer until an independently designed IR transitive-retention API is owner-approved. **Recommend A:** only five consumed physical payloads need protection, with existing direct lineage and currentness rules; no new public lifetime guarantee.
**Q5 — persistence.** A: dependency-ready lookup plus guarded genuine PersistentPrefix writes. B: read-only first, with writes deferred. **Recommend A:** automatic cold/warm evidence and whole-closure refusal remain observable; B requires a separately authorized genuine producer before positive cache-hit evidence, with the same independent expectations.
